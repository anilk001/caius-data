# Email 2 to Boldata LLC — requesting the agreement before paying

Sent after their 16 September answers (summarised in STATUS.md). Those answers
were clear and favourable; this email does not re-ask any of them. It asks for
the document that makes them binding, and settles the commercial and coverage
points we need before choosing a credit pack.

Order matters: **agreement first, payment second.** They offered to agree terms
before purchase, and the setup fee is non-refundable in the general case.

---

**Subject:** Ready to proceed — please send the reseller agreement for signature

Hi,

Thank you for the detailed answers on 16 September. They covered what we needed
on resale, storage, survival and territory, and we are ready to move forward.

We have now completed the free trial and built our pipeline against your API, so
the next step is the paid account. Before we pay the setup fee, please send the
written agreement for us to review and sign — as you offered. A few points we
would like to see reflected in it, or clarified in your reply:

**The agreement**

1. The resale permission as you described it: resale in raw or derived form, no
   cap on customers or records, permanent storage, and continued use of records
   already purchased if we later stop buying credits.
2. The indemnity for third-party claims arising from your data sources — your
   earlier answer said "subject to the agreed contractual terms", so we would
   like to read the clause itself.
3. Term, renewal and termination: what notice applies on either side, and what
   happens to unused credits on termination.

**The setup fee**

4. What the $499 covers, and whether it is one-off or recurs annually.
5. Whether it is refundable in any circumstance — for example if coverage turns
   out not to include a sector we have relied on.

**Credits**

6. We expect to start with the $199 / 200,000-credit pack. Please confirm the
   current price list, the 12-month validity, and that a later purchase extends
   the expiry on the earlier balance.
7. Is there a volume discount or annual plan worth considering at 1,000,000
   credits or above?

**Coverage — we need this before choosing a pack size**

8. Which HS chapters does your US import data cover? We are planning packs for
   spices (HS 09), leather goods (HS 4202/4203), garments (HS 61/62) and
   pharmaceuticals (HS 3003/3004), all US imports from India.
9. How complete is US import coverage, how soon after arrival does a record
   appear, and how often is it refreshed?
10. Do the aggregated importer records (15 credits) ever carry city and state,
    or is that only on shipment records via `consignee_address`? Our packs are
    more valuable with a US location, so this affects which endpoint we buy.

**One thing from the trial**

11. Our free trial stopped at 500 shipping records with HTTP 402, not the 1,000
    credits advertised. Not a complaint — we would just like to understand the
    accounting before we size a paid pack, in case shipping records are billed
    differently from the headline rate.

**Invoicing**

12. Please invoice Caius Data LLC (United States). Our EIN is being issued now;
    we will send it as soon as it arrives. Which payment methods do you accept?

Happy to sign an NDA first if that helps with any of the above.
