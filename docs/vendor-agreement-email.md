# Email 2 to Boldata LLC — agreement first, then the order

Sent after their 16 September answers (summarised in STATUS.md). Those answers
were clear and favourable; this email does not re-ask any of them. It commits to
the order so the request is commercial rather than exploratory, and asks for the
document that makes their answers binding.

Order matters: **agreement signed first, payment second.** They offered to agree
terms before purchase, and the setup fee is non-refundable in the general case.
The email says plainly that the money follows signature, which is the fastest
honest way to get a document out of a vendor.

The three questions that actually change what we buy are 8, 9 and 10:
city/state on importer records, coverage of the four sectors, and the trial
accounting.

---

**Subject:** Ready to sign and order — please send the reseller agreement

Hi,

Thank you for the detailed answers on 16 September. They covered what we needed
on resale, storage, survival and territory, and we would like to proceed.

To be clear about where we are: we have completed the free trial, built and
tested our pipeline against your API, and our company and bank account are now
set up. **As soon as the agreement is signed we will pay the $499 API setup fee
and purchase the $199 / 200,000-credit pack**, with larger packs to follow as our
customer base grows.

Please send the reseller agreement for review and signature. A few points we
would like to see reflected in it, or clarified in your reply:

**The agreement**

1. The resale permission as you described it: resale in raw or derived form, no
   cap on customers or records, permanent storage, and continued use of records
   already purchased if we later stop buying credits.
2. The indemnity for third-party claims arising from your data sources. Your
   earlier answer said "subject to the agreed contractual terms", so we would
   like to read the clause itself.
3. Term, renewal and termination: what notice applies on either side, and what
   happens to unused credits on termination.

**The setup fee**

4. What the $499 covers, and whether it is one-off or recurs annually.
5. Whether it is refundable in any circumstance — for example if coverage turns
   out not to include a sector we have relied on.

**Credits**

6. Please confirm the current price list, the 12-month validity, and that a
   later purchase extends the expiry on the earlier balance.
7. Is there a volume discount or annual plan worth considering at 1,000,000
   credits or above? We expect to reach that range if the first packs sell.

**Coverage — this decides which endpoint we buy from**

8. Do the aggregated importer records (15 credits) ever carry city and state, or
   is a US location only available on shipment records via `consignee_address`?
   Our packs are considerably more valuable with a city and state in them, so
   this changes how we spend the credits.
9. Which HS chapters does your US import data cover? We are planning packs for
   spices (HS 09), leather goods (HS 4202/4203), garments (HS 61/62) and
   pharmaceuticals (HS 3003/3004), all US imports from India.
10. How complete is US import coverage, how soon after arrival does a record
    appear, and how often is it refreshed?

**One thing from the trial**

11. Our free trial stopped at 500 shipping records with an HTTP 402, rather than
    the 1,000 credits advertised. Not a complaint — we would just like to
    understand the accounting before sizing a paid pack, in case shipping
    records are billed differently from the headline rate.

**Invoicing**

12. Please invoice Caius Data LLC (United States). Our EIN is being issued now
    and we will send it as soon as it arrives. Which payment methods do you
    accept?

Happy to sign an NDA first if that helps with any of the above. If it is easier
to cover these on a call, we can make ourselves available at your convenience.
