# Email 3 to the vendor — the indemnity gap, before signature

The counterparty on the Master Data Reseller & API Services Agreement is
**Digital Media Solutions AU Pty Ltd**, trading as BillOfLadingData.com
(Melbourne, Victoria), not Boldata LLC as their documentation footer suggests.
Governing law is Victoria, Australia.

The agreement's resale terms are strong and in places better than their email
promised — §5.6 in particular keeps purchased data usable after credits expire,
after a subscription ends and after termination. That is the clause the business
depends on and it is explicit.

One clause contradicts what they put in writing on 16 September, and it is the
one we relied on. Their email said they warrant upstream rights and would
indemnify for claims arising from their data sources. §17.2 indemnifies only
against claims that *Provider's own software, API or documentation* infringes
IP, and then expressly excludes "Third-Party Data; public records; government
Data" — which is the entire product.

Read with §15.1(3) ("to its knowledge") and the §18.2 cap at fees paid, the
position is that a claim from a data source falls on the Reseller, with recovery
limited to roughly the setup fee.

This email asks them to reconcile the two. It deliberately does not raise the
entity question or the unused-credit question — one ask, clearly made, gets a
faster answer than four.

---

**Subject:** Reseller Agreement — one clause to reconcile before we sign

Hi,

Thank you for sending the Master Data Reseller & API Services Agreement. We
have read it in full. The resale terms are clear and workable, and Section 5.6
in particular — permanent use of purchased data after credits expire or the
agreement ends — is exactly the basis we need to build on. We are ready to sign
and to pay the setup fee and the 200,000-credit pack as quoted.

Before we do, there is one point we need to reconcile, because it is the point
we relied on when we decided to proceed.

In your email of 16 September you confirmed:

> you warrant that you hold the necessary upstream rights, and would indemnify
> us against claims brought by your data sources.

Section 17.2 of the agreement reads differently. It indemnifies the Reseller
only against a claim that *Provider's proprietary software, API technology or
documentation* infringes a third party's intellectual property, and then
expressly excludes claims resulting from "Third-Party Data; public records;
government Data".

Since the trade data itself is what we license and resell, that exclusion
removes the protection your email described. Read together with Section 15.1(3),
where the rights warranty is qualified "to its knowledge", and the Section 18.2
cap at fees paid, the effect is that a claim from one of your data sources would
sit with us.

We are not asking you to warrant the accuracy or completeness of the data —
Sections 9 and 16 are entirely reasonable and we accept them. We are asking only
that you stand behind your right to license it to us for resale, which is what
your email said and what Section 5 sells.

Either of these would resolve it for us:

1. An amendment to Section 17.2 extending the Provider indemnity to third-party
   claims that Provider did not hold the rights necessary to license the Data to
   Reseller for resale as permitted under Section 5; or
2. A short signed side letter confirming the position in your 16 September
   email, stated to prevail over Section 17.2 to the extent of any conflict.

Option 2 is probably quicker for both of us, and we are happy to work from your
wording.

This is the only item holding up signature. Everything else in the agreement we
accept as drafted.

Best regards,
